Route Viewer
    --

    Drop a location log

    Drag a file anywhere, or choose one.

    Data format

    Route Viewer reads a plain text file with one reading per line, separated by commas:

    date time, label, id, latitude, longitude, address

    Example

    2025-03-14 08:15:03,Phone,1,37.774929,-122.419418,1 Market St, San Francisco, CA
2025-03-14 08:15:33,Phone,1,37.775110,-122.418700,Market St, San Francisco, CA
    Date and time
    YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS A T instead of the space works too, and fractional seconds are fine. Read as local time.
    Label, ID
    Any text without commas. They are ignored, but both fields must be there.
    Latitude, longitude
    Decimal degrees, like 37.774929 and -122.419418.
    Address
    Optional, and may contain commas. Shown in popups and searchable.

    Lines that don't match (headers, blanks) are skipped, and readings are sorted by time automatically. Accepts .txt, .csv and .log. Your file stays in your browser; only map tiles are loaded from the internet.
